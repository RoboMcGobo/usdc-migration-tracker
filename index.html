Method

Counterparties come from the Cosmos chain registry _IBC/*noble*.json files (63 at build time), plus XPLA, which routes USDC through the Cosmos Hub rather than a direct Noble channel. USDC.n on chain is the bank supply of ibc/sha256("transfer/<channel>/uusdc") on that chain's own REST endpoint, direct one-hop from Noble only. USDC.inj on chain is the same for erc20:0xa00C…235a sent from Injective (XPLA: two hops via the Hub).

Changes over time use the IBC escrow accounts: the uusdc balance of Noble's escrow for each counterparty channel equals the USDC.n outstanding through that channel, and Noble's public archive nodes answer height-pinned queries back past September 1. Injective's public nodes keep roughly one week of state, so USDC.inj changes beyond 7 days come from this page's own daily snapshot store. Escrow counts tokens forwarded onward through the counterparty, so it can exceed the on-chain supply; the detail view shows both.

Injective IBC status is OPEN only when the registry lists a transfer channel and both ends report STATE_OPEN. The threshold uses the larger of on-chain supply and Noble escrow, so a chain whose endpoints are down is not silently dropped. Current figures are fetched live in your browser from public endpoints; daily history is committed to data/snapshots.json by a scheduled GitHub Action, so every visitor sees the same series.
